US GDP Growth Accelerates to 3.4% in Q3; Inflation Also Rises +

GDP grew at a 3.4% annualized rate in Q3, beating expectations and driven by strong business investment (up 12.7%) and consumer spending (up 3.8%), with AI equipment purchases surging 20.9%. However, inflation accelerated on both monthly and annual bases, and the trade deficit widened due to overseas chip purchases, partially offsetting GDP gains. Rising mortgage rates also dampened homebuilding.

Why it matters: Stronger GDP supports commercial real estate leasing demand and cap rate stability, but persistent inflation and higher rates pressure construction costs and cap rates—a mixed signal requiring close monitoring of Fed policy and interest rate trajectory.

Perry's Cafe Diner to Housing: 223-Unit Development Underway in San Diego's Old Town +

Zephyr is converting the historic Perry's Cafe in Old Town San Diego into a 223-unit mixed-income apartment complex (7 stories), targeting completion in early 2028 with 33 units reserved for affordability. The project is positioned as momentum-builder for the adjacent Midway Rising development, which awaits City Council approval. This represents a broader regional trend of adaptive reuse driving housing supply.

Why it matters: This directly impacts San Diego's housing supply strategy and demonstrates how preservation-focused development can achieve density—critical context for understanding the city's development pipeline and approval timelines.

LA Homebuyers Propping Up San Diego Real Estate Prices; Equity Migration Reshaping Market +

Los Angeles residents represent the largest cohort of out-of-market home searchers in San Diego (11.7% vs. 2.3% from San Francisco), leveraging home equity gains to purchase larger properties in premium neighborhoods like Rancho Santa Fe. Real estate agents report LA buyers view San Diego properties as significantly discounted relative to comparable LA homes, artificially supporting local price levels. Limited local housing supply combined with external capital demand is creating a structural pricing floor.

Why it matters: This reveals a critical market dynamic: San Diego pricing sustainability depends on external capital flows from higher-priced markets; understanding this equity-migration pattern is essential for long-term investment thesis and exit strategy planning.

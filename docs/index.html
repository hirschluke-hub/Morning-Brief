New York Fed research found remote work is responsible for 64% of the rise in college graduate unemployment (ages <29) since 2019, with jobless rates up 200 bps among 'remotable' job categories. Why it matters: If youth unemployment is driven by WFH adoption rather than AI displacement, this suggests structural demand destruction in office leasing may be more durable than some recovery scenarios assume—critical for office portfolio underwriting.

A Municipal Employees Association poll shows 69% of San Diegans believe the city is on the wrong track (up from 49% YoY), with housing costs named the top concern by 24% of respondents. However, 50% support higher taxes/fees to fund affordable housing and community improvements.

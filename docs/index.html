Construction Costs Hit Fastest Annual Growth Since the Pandemic at 9.6% Year-Over-Year +

Construction input prices surged 9.6% year-over-year in May, the fastest growth since the pandemic, driven by tariffs, energy costs, and commodity spikes—particularly copper wire/cable (up 24.2% YoY) and steel (up 7%). This margin squeeze is hitting developers already burdened by elevated financing costs.

Why it matters: As a development and acquisitions analyst, you need to model these cost inflation trends carefully into underwriting assumptions and capital planning, especially as tariff policies remain in flux.

Federal Government's Real Estate Strategy Faces $26B Maintenance Backlog Problem +

The Trump administration's push to divest excess federal real estate is hitting headwinds from aging buildings and a massive maintenance backlog that must be addressed before properties can be sold. The $26 billion deferred maintenance problem is slowing disposition timelines and creating uncertainty in the federal real estate market.

Why it matters: Federal property dispositions can create unique acquisition opportunities for investors with renovation capital—understand the backlog constraints to identify timing windows and negotiate with motivated sellers.

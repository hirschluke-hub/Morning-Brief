Russian Word of the Day
Говорите медленнее, пожалуйста
guh-vah-REE-tye MED-len-nay-yuh, pah-ZHAHL-stuh
Please speak more slowly
Explanation +
This is an essential phrase for beginners when native speakers talk too fast. 'Говорите' (speak) is the formal/plural imperative form, 'медленнее' (more slowly) is the comparative form of the adjective. Use this whenever you need someone to slow down their speech so you can process the words. Example: 'Говорите медленнее, я только начинаю учить русский' (Speak more slowly, I'm just beginning to learn Russian).

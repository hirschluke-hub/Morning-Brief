Russian Word of the Day
Сколько это стоит?
SKOL-ka EH-ta STO-it?
How much does this cost?
Explanation +
This is the most essential phrase for shopping in Russian. Use it whenever you want to know the price of an item. The stress falls on the first syllable of 'сколько' and the second syllable of 'стоит'. Example: Сколько это стоит? (How much does this cost?) — You'll use this phrase constantly when shopping at markets or stores.

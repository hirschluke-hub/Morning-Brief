Russian Word of the Day
Сколько это стоит?
SKOL-ka EH-ta STO-it?
How much does this cost?
Explanation +
This is the most essential question to ask when shopping in Russian. Use it whenever you want to know the price of an item. The word 'сколько' means 'how much,' 'это' means 'this,' and 'стоит' means 'costs.' Example: Сколько это стоит в магазине? (How much does this cost in the store?)

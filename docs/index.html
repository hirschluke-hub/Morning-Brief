Multifamily Starts Plunge 42% as Financing Pressures Mount +

U.S. multifamily housing starts collapsed 41.6% in May to 284,000 units, driven by financing math that no longer works for developers. The dramatic pullback signals that current lending conditions are forcing project cancellations and delays despite underlying housing demand.

Why it matters: This is the canary in the coal mine for development pipelines—understanding why deals won't pencil is critical for identifying where capital will flow and what development opportunities emerge from the wreckage.

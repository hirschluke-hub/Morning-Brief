Russian Word of the Day
горячий
ga-RYA-chee-y
hot
Explanation +
Горячий describes something that is hot to the touch or temperature-wise. It's commonly used for hot water, hot food, or hot weather. The word changes form based on gender and case: горячий (masculine), горячая (feminine), горячее (neuter). Example: Вода очень горячая. (The water is very hot.)

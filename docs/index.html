Russian Word of the Day
Сколько это стоит?
SKOL-ka EH-ta STO-it?
How much does this cost?
Explanation +
This is the most essential question when shopping in Russian. Use it whenever you want to know the price of an item. The phrase breaks down as: Сколько (how much) + это (this) + стоит (costs). You'll hear this constantly in markets and shops, and your girlfriend will likely appreciate you asking instead of guessing! Example: "Сколько это стоит?" (How much does this cost?)

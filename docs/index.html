Miami Now More Expensive Than NYC; Housing Costs Up 79% Since Pandemic +

Greater Miami has surpassed New York as the most expensive U.S. metro area for the first time, with consumer prices up 36% since 2019 and housing up 79%, driven by high-income transplants and pandemic migration. Property taxes jumped 62%, while new Surfside-inspired condo safety requirements add ongoing carrying costs. The shift undermines Miami's original value proposition for relocating NYC residents and businesses.

Why it matters: This is a critical market-timing signal for acquisitions, development, and investor positioning—Miami's arbitrage advantage is closing, affecting both residential and commercial CRE strategy.

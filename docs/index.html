Goldman Sachs Launches Tokenized Real Estate Fund +

Goldman Sachs is launching a blockchain-native real estate fund in partnership with Apex Group and Archax, leveraging its GS DAP platform to tokenize real estate investments. This marks significant institutional validation of blockchain-based property ownership, enabling fractional ownership and improved settlement efficiency on the blockchain.

Why it matters: Tokenization is reshaping how institutional capital flows into real estate—understanding blockchain-native investment vehicles is essential as you evaluate emerging proptech and alternative capital sources.

Real Estate-Backed Candidates See Mixed Success In LA, Statewide Votes +

Real estate industry funding had mixed results in California primaries—Karen Bass and Spencer Pratt both received industry support in the LA mayoral race, while San Jose Mayor Matt Mahan (backed by ~$1.6M from Rick Caruso) lost statewide in the governor's race. Developer Zach Sokoloff also lost his LA city controller bid despite industry backing, signaling limits to real estate lobby influence on housing and fiscal policy issues.

Why it matters: Understanding policy headwinds and the effectiveness of real estate industry advocacy helps you assess regulatory risk in development and acquisition decisions—especially critical for San Diego projects sensitive to zoning and housing policy.

Sale-Leasebacks Surge As Companies Eye Ways To Raise Cash +

Sale-leaseback activity is accelerating as corporates seek to unlock balance sheet liquidity amid broader capital constraints and economic uncertainty.

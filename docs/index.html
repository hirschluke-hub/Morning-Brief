Russian Word of the Day
Как пройти до...?
Kak prah-TI do...?
How do I get to...?
Explanation +
This is one of the most practical phrases for navigating any city. Use it when you need directions to a specific location. The phrase is polite and clear, and locals will immediately understand you're asking for directions. Example: "Как пройти до метро?" (How do I get to the metro?)
